About Me

Policy Advocate & Changemaker

I am a dedicated policy advocate, social entrepreneur, and changemaker with over 20 years of experience in gender equity, education, and citizen leadership training. My work focuses on creating impactful social reforms, empowering marginalized communities, and implementing sustainable development initiatives. I strive to build policies that ensure inclusion, safety, and equitable opportunities for all.

Career Beginnings

I bring over two decades of professional experience. I began my career as a Project Manager, leading large IT and SEZ construction projects. Later, as the CSR Head at an architectural consultancy, I led educational initiatives for marginalized children of construction workers, religious minorities, Dalit and Adivasi, reaching nearly 10,000 students.
